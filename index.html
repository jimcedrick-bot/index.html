COMMUNITY PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION

Every neighborhood across the Philippines has its own unique story, but look closer and you will find common, everyday struggles that shape local life. To truly understand these community issues, our research combines the power of numerical data with real human stories. By blending quantitative surveys that map out the scale of the problems with qualitative interviews that capture the lived experiences of residents, we use a mixed-methods approach to see the complete picture.


We do not just collect numbers or record words; we listen to the voices behind every specific struggle and give meaning to the patterns we observe. This bridges the gap between facts and faces, turning cold figures into real people with real lives, and turning personal accounts into insights that can guide meaningful change. Ultimately, this approach creates a balanced, deeply human look at what our communities go through, allowing us to build better solutions together—solutions that are rooted in what people actually need, designed with their input, and built to uplift the very neighborhoods we call our own.
